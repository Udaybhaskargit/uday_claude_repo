# E2-S2 — Load deductible, co-pay, and decision threshold configuration

**Epic:** E2 — Business Rule Configuration
**Layer:** Config
**Group:** B
**Depends on:** E1-S1

## Description

Externalize the per-claim-type deductible/co-pay formulas and the decision thresholds (auto-approve ceiling, zero-payable rule) as configuration consumed by the assessment and decision services.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given config/assessment-rules.json, when loaded, then motor has deductible 5000 and co_pay_pct 0, health has deductible 1000 and co_pay_pct 10, life has deductible 0 and co_pay_pct 0.
2. Given the decision config, when loaded, then auto_approve_ceiling equals 50000.
3. Given the config, when a claim_type outside {motor, health, life} is requested, then the loader raises a typed UnknownClaimTypeError.
