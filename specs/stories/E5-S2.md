# E5-S2 — Implement the deterministic fraud scoring engine

**Epic:** E5 — Document Verification & Fraud Screening
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** E
**Depends on:** E2-S1, E3-S3

## Description

Compute a deterministic fraud score by summing the weights of every triggered rule from the E2-S1 config and comparing to the configured threshold. This is AC-04.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a claim with a claim_amount/sum_insured ratio of 0.85 and no other rule triggers, when scored, then the total is 40 and flagged is False, since 40 is below threshold 60.
2. Given a claim filed 2 days after policy inception with a claim_amount/sum_insured ratio of 0.85, when scored, then the total is 65 (40+25) and flagged is True.
3. Given the same claim inputs scored twice, when the two results are compared, then both runs produce an identical score and rule breakdown, demonstrating determinism.
4. Given a motor claim missing the POLICE_FIR document, when scored, then the +15 rule is included in the breakdown with its rule name.
